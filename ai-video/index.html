关于本栏
About This Section

创作空间 · Creative Space

本栏目并不以“信息传递”为主要目的，而更接近于一种视觉与听觉的创作空间。音乐不只是背景，影像也不仅是说明，它们在时间中彼此作用，构成一种新的表达方式。

This section is not intended as an informational channel, but as a creative audiovisual space. Music is not merely background, and images are not just illustration—they interact through time to form a new mode of expression.

创作说明 · Creative Notes

这些作品通常从一个声音或一段节奏开始，通过影像与时间的重组逐步展开。AI在其中并不是替代创作，而是作为一种工具参与到视觉生成与结构构建之中。

These works often begin with a sound or rhythm, gradually unfolding through visual sequencing and temporal structure. AI does not replace creation, but participates as a tool in generating imagery and shaping composition.

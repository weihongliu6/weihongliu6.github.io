Privacy & visitor analytics

由你选择 / Your choice

基础访问次数使用无 Cookie、无访客标识的匿名页面计数；此前拒绝统计的选择仍受尊重。详细操作统计默认为关闭。点击本页底部“隐私与统计”即可允许或拒绝，稍后也可撤回。我们在此浏览器保存你的选择六个月。拒绝不会影响阅读、下载、图片或音乐。

Basic page counting uses no cookies or visitor identifiers and honours previous declines. Optional interaction analytics remain off until you allow them. Use “Privacy & analytics” on this page to allow, decline or withdraw. Your choice stays on this browser for six months. All content works without analytics.

最少的数据 / Minimal data

同意后，我们使用现有的 GoatCounter 服务，记录规范化页面名称、作品或章节代号、操作类别、外部来源网站域名及预先批准的推广标签。只区分手机、平板与桌面宽度类别。不会发送网址查询参数、完整来源网址、表单内容、姓名、电子邮件或阅读笔记，不收集完整 IP 地址，也不使用指纹或跨站访客标识。

After consent, we send canonical content paths, interaction categories, referring website origins and approved campaign labels to GoatCounter. Device reporting uses coarse viewport classes. We do not send query strings, full referrer URLs, form values, names, emails or notes. IP-based sessions, browser details and geolocation are disabled. No fingerprinting or cross-site identifier is used. The network provider necessarily handles an IP address to deliver requests; our analytics do not retain it.

统计不是阅读证明 / What events mean

打开书籍或章节不代表读完。文章“阅读深度”表示页面到达正文 75%，并累计约 30 秒可见、有焦点且近期有操作的时间，不证明理解或读完。音乐只统计用户操作后播放器实际开始播放；外部平台链接只记为点击。下载只记录发起点击，不保证文件完整保存。

Book/chapter opens are not completions. Reading depth means reaching 75% of article height plus an estimated 30 seconds of visible, focused, recently active time; it is not proof of reading. A playback start requires a visitor action and the player’s actual playing event. External platform clicks are not listens. Download clicks do not confirm completed transfers.

浏览器本地记录 / Local records

允许统计后，本机只保存一个当天日期以避免重复计算日浏览器数，以及短期事件去重标记；不会生成或传送访客 ID。日浏览器数不等于人数，跨日相加是“浏览器天数”，不是月独立人数。共享设备、清除存储、拒绝统计或拦截脚本都会影响估算。主题、字号和阅读位置等原有本地功能独立运行。

With consent, this browser keeps a day marker and short-lived event deduplication markers, never a visitor ID. Daily browsers are estimates; adding days produces browser-days, not monthly unique people. Shared devices, storage clearing, blockers and declined consent affect coverage. Existing reading preferences and position storage remain separate.

统计仅供站主登录查看，保留现有历史汇总；主要页面仅显示简洁的累计访问次数。GoatCounter 的数据在芬兰和德国保存，不共享给其他第三方。
Detailed statistics are private to signed-in authorised users; main pages show only a simple aggregate visits badge. Existing historical aggregates are retained. GoatCounter hosts data in Finland and Germany.

在你用来管理网站的每个浏览器操作一次。此设置不会改变其他人的访问。
Use this on each browser you use to manage or test the site. It only changes this browser.
